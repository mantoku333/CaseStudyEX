# Graceful Movement Trail

## Four-Level Revision

The current prefab has fourteen Particle Systems: five sprite layers, three
live ribbons and six pooled fade systems. `PlayerMovementTrail` reads
`PlayerElegantPointController.CurrentChainLength` directly, without requiring
`FX_Kirakira` or its manager. Level 0 stops new births; levels above 4 use the
level-4 appearance. Existing particles retain their lifetime on settlement.
The legacy manager's `useLegacySparkles` defaults to false.

Level 1 keeps the understated base. Level 2 adds a delayed overlapping ribbon
and pale pink/cyan birth colors. Level 3 introduces a soft third veil. Level 4
widens the layered flow and spread, while fine sparkle density reaches 2.15x
and petal density only 1.85x. Brightness is not multiplied by level. Parameter
changes interpolate over 0.45 seconds per level without restarting systems.
At all levels, ribbons are intermittent and release into pooled fading systems.

`PetalBroad.png` is an original generated transparent sprite, created with
OpenAI image generation for this revision: one broad asymmetrical pearlescent
cherry blossom petal with a small cleft and curved base. It replaces the narrow
petal in the material; the original image is retained. Petals are 0.42-0.72
world units at level 1, with restrained out-of-plane rotation to prevent thin
edge-on silhouettes. The shader adds a multi-radius soft edge glow.

Verification now also renders `Level1.png` through `Level4.png`, tests smooth
level transitions, natural expiry at level 0 while moving, the level-4 cap,
and direct point-controller integration with no legacy FX manager present.
The implementation still uses the existing gauge gain notification and fallback
absorption effect (now pastel); the new ribbon mesh itself is not bent into the HUD.

The notes below describe the earlier single-level iteration.

Installed in `Assets/Scenes/FixScenes/Fuyuno_Fix_UI.unity` at
`Player/FX_Trail/GracefulTrail`. The original placeholder is retained as a
container with its old particle emission and renderer disabled.

Prefab: `Assets/Prefabs/Effects/FX_GracefulMovementTrail.prefab`.
Five sprite layers provide fine sparkles, pearl-like motes, star glints, petals,
and soft glow. Native Particle System ribbons appear as short intermittent
strokes, with one stroke in most bursts and a delayed second in every third
burst. There are eleven Particle Systems including four pooled fade systems.

`GracefulParticleRibbon` resamples the movement history as a smooth curve,
adds coherent sway, and supplies ordered vertices to the native Ribbon module.
The ribbon uses a continuous pastel spectrum and an analytic silk-like profile,
without repeated sprite images or independent random vertex noise.
Each stroke grows for 0.46 s (secondary: 0.299 s), then releases its shape to a
pooled Particle System. Unity handles its remaining lifetime and alpha curve:
0.46 s for the main stroke, 0.34 s for the accent. Bursts start approximately
1.0-1.1 s apart, leaving clear intervals without ribbons. Soft transparent tips
avoid hard cuts and subpixel dotted ends. Old strokes never join new paths.

Sizes are calibrated to the player sprite's scene bounds when rebuilding.
Main/secondary maximum ribbon widths are 2.5%/1.4% of that height before tapering.
At 7 world units/s the main stroke grows about 3.2 units, approximately 23 times
its maximum width with the target player's scale. Motes are 6-11%, star sprites
11-18%, and fine sparkles 2-4%. Their transparent margins make the visible cores
smaller. Emitters sit 16% of the sprite height below the existing FX_Trail anchor
to keep the light around the torso instead of the umbrella.
Petals retain their 0.65-1.05 world-unit size.

Default behavior: emit during movement at every graceful-action level.
Action-only gating is intentionally disabled for this visual-tuning iteration.
`Only During Graceful Actions` can be enabled later for glide/dodge/dive/recoil.
`Minimum Graceful Level` on `PlayerMovementTrail` can be set to 1 or 2 to gate
the trail using the existing `PlayerGracefulActionEffectManager`.
The 0.06 s stop delay bridges non-interpolated 50 Hz physics updates.
Stopping only disables emission: no lifetime shortening, particle replacement,
or blanket clear. Sprite lifetimes range from 0.4 to 1.6 s, with individual
fade curves, so fine lights fade first and petals linger. Only disabling the
whole effect or teleporting clears old particles immediately.

## Textures and Materials

- `Petal.png`: original asset generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool.
  Transparent RGBA source retained; imported at 256 px without compression.
- `PastelSpectrum.png`: near-white pearl pink, lavender, sky, mint, pale yellow,
  and white palette, generated by `GracefulTrailBuilder`. All RGB channels are
  at least 0.93, keeping rainbow differences subordinate to the white light.
- `GracefulRibbon.shader`: continuous ribbon profile; `IridescentRibbon.mat`
  uses this shader and the spectrum above.
- `Pearl.png`: soft radial light mask with a small bright core for motes and fine sparkles.
- `Star.png`: code-generated four-point glint with a soft central halo.
- `Glow.png`: code-generated radial soft light mask.
- Four dedicated materials use `GameName/FX/Graceful Particle`. Texture saturation
  is reduced to 15% so the pink in the petal art does not override the unified
  palette. Brightness and opacity are balanced per layer; broad halos are faint.
  The existing shared sparkle shader is unchanged. No extra package is required.

## Editing and Verification

Adjust sprite Particle Systems directly. Ribbon width/sway/fade are on
`GracefulParticleRibbon`; cadence and duration are on `PlayerMovementTrail`.
`Tools > Effects > Rebuild Graceful Trail Prefab` restores the authored defaults
and regenerates the mathematical masks and palette; it does not replace the petal art.
`Install Graceful Movement Trail` rebuilds and installs into the target scene,
then saves that scene, including any current scene edits.

`Tools > Effects > Verify Graceful Movement Trail` runs deterministic editor
simulations and captures dark/light-background renders under
`Temp/GracefulTrailVerification`. When the target scene is loaded, it also
captures the current player sprite at its scene scale. Checks cover material
and shader validity, world-space simulation, idle, movement, stop/fade,
restart, teleport, disable/re-enable, pause, 144 fps with 50 Hz physics, and
intervals with zero ribbons between bursts of one or two. Tests compare live
particles before/after release to ensure neither lifetime nor particle count
is rewritten, and confirm fine sparkles persist between ribbon bursts.
One hundred twenty 30 fps frames capture two seconds moving and two stopped.
`SceneGameplayScale.png` and `SceneCloseup.png` also render a temporary effect
against the loaded scene through URP, using the actual camera's zoom for the
first image. Temporary objects are destroyed without saving the scene.
These are editor simulations and scene renders, not a full gameplay session.

Unity reference: [Particle System TrailModule](https://docs.unity3d.com/ja/2022.3/ScriptReference/ParticleSystem.TrailModule.html).
